Earn Open Data

Strategies for small companies and stewards to fundraise through meaningful open data work.

Introduction

This site explores earning income via open data stewardship: maintaining datasets, portals, and tools for community benefit. Focus on sustainable models for small teams, drawing from open source best practices.

Key Stewardship Opportunities

  • Data Maintenance: Curate, validate, and update open datasets. Small cos can offer ongoing stewardship contracts.
  • Portal Operations: Run open data platforms with fiscal sponsorship (e.g., NumFOCUS model).
  • Tooling & Guides: Build/maintain analysis tools, following OSS funding guides.
  • Community Building: Facilitate events, docs; seek recurring sponsorships over VC highs.

  • Getting Paid for OSS: Recurring donations, fiscal hosts for sustainability.
